# Codex–Claude Career Handoff

Use this packet whenever work moves from Codex to Claude or back. It keeps the assistants aligned on evidence rather than on whichever chat contains the most polished wording.

```text
Career objective or target hypothesis:
[Roles, level, sectors, and constraints. Mark assumptions.]

Professional narrative in progress:
[Two to four sentences, with wording that remains provisional.]

Confirmed evidence:
- [Contribution, result, and the artifact or source that supports it.]

Candidate evidence that needs confirmation:
- [Possible contribution, originating chat or project, and confirmation needed.]

Assistant-derived leads or judgment-profile hypotheses:
- [Pattern or possible story, source context, and the example needed to test it.]

Confidentiality boundaries:
- [Details that cannot appear in external materials.]

Current deliverable:
[Career map, resume draft, target comparison, LinkedIn profile, or interview stories.]

Specific request for this assistant:
[The next focused task.]
```

For a Claude project, paste the packet into the project’s instructions or the first working chat, then attach or summarize the actual source materials it may use. Ask Claude to preserve the confidence labels and to flag any wording that exceeds the evidence.

For Codex, add the same packet to the active task along with the relevant files or chat exports. Do not paste confidential transcripts in full simply to create a record. A short source-grounded summary and a reference to the original material are usually enough.

## Mining old AI work responsibly

Work through old projects one at a time. Start with the project or set of chats most likely to contain a visible work product. For each useful thread, capture the project name, approximate date, the underlying work completed, the resulting artifact or decision, and the evidence still required. Discard tool chatter, speculative brainstorming, and repeated drafts that did not become work.

An AI conversation can reliably show that the candidate shaped a problem, created an artifact, or drove a decision process when the conversation and resulting material support that account. It does not independently prove implementation, adoption, or organizational impact.
