## Evidence rules

The evidence ledger uses four labels. Preserve them exactly.

| Label | Meaning | External use |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | A dated artifact, official role record, decision record, published item, metric, or corroborated feedback supports the statement. | Potentially usable after a claim-level safety check. |
| **Detailed account** | Doug can describe the situation, contribution, output, and limits from direct experience, with no independent record attached yet. | Pressure-test and narrow before using. |
| **Lead** | A memory, partial source, chat, profile, calendar item, or other discovery clue points to something worth recovering. | Never use as a claim. |
| **Do not use externally** | Information is sensitive, confidential, incomplete, or too personal for a public narrative. | Keep as private context only. |

For every prospective claim, identify Doug's distinct contribution, the concrete output or decision, the strongest evidence basis, and the public-safety boundary. Do not infer outcomes from silent distribution, participation, access, or a system existing after the fact.
