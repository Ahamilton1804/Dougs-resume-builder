# DPI-005: iPad Asset Control and Remediation

## Working account

Doug reports that, across much of 2025 and the preceding period, he spent substantial time on an inherited iPad-fleet problem. He describes fragmented ownership, an aging fleet, devices that could not be located, ongoing line and device costs, and no coherent operating process. He reports helping size the problem, locate and clean up devices, select a vendor-managed service model, and define a more accountable ordering, inventory, training, and return process.

The local project records establish the control problem and show Doug assigned to several operational and transition responsibilities. They do not establish that he alone led the project, the ultimate fleet outcome, realized savings, the reported total loss, or the reported age profile of the fleet.

## Career interpretation

This is an operational-governance and remediation story. It belongs alongside the implementation chapter's demand system, product function, people development, continuity work, and adoption model. Its transferable pattern is: diagnose an inherited control failure, reconcile records, define ownership and approval flows, coordinate a vendor transition, and put recurring asset-management work into an operating process.

The safe external framing is still limited. A future resume or interview claim could describe leading or co-leading a cross-functional mobile-device remediation only after the accountability model and completed transition are corroborated. Until then, this is strongest as a private interview example of judgment under operational ambiguity.

## Evidence boundary

- Keep raw inventory records, device identifiers, phone numbers, IMEIs, asset details, carrier data, vendor terms, financial amounts, state information, and staff names private.
- Do not claim a dollar loss, a number of lost devices, final savings, a completed vendor transition, or a final modernization outcome from the current records.
- Treat the reported obsolete second-generation iPad fleet as Doug's detailed account until an inventory or refresh record establishes model distribution and condition.
