# Career Evidence Ledger

This is the source of truth for career claims. A resume, LinkedIn profile, or interview story should be drawn from this record, not created independently.

## Evidence labels

- **Confirmed**: supported by a finished artifact, official role record, performance feedback, a decision record, a published item, a metric, or a person who can confirm it.
- **Detailed account**: Doug can describe the situation, contribution, output, and limits from direct experience, but no independent record is currently attached.
- **Lead**: a chat, workspace memory, judgment profile, calendar item, partial draft, or vague recollection identifies a story worth recovering.
- **Do not use externally**: true or useful context that is confidential, too incomplete, or otherwise unsuitable for a public-facing claim.

A detailed account is legitimate evidence of Doug’s experience. It should be named honestly and pressure-tested before becoming a precise external claim. A lead is never evidence by itself.

## Claim check

Before a line moves into a resume, confirm that it is literally true, distinguishes Doug’s work from the team’s work, uses the strongest available basis, and could withstand a question from a knowledgeable colleague. Narrow the language when evidence is incomplete.
