The June 23 Outlook message titled `Vibe Coding` documents Doug's decision to create guidance for staff using generative AI to build tools. It identifies the practical problems the guidance was meant to prevent: a creator unable to explain the resulting project, absent scope and documentation, work misaligned with priorities, uncontrolled scope expansion, missing coordination, insufficient legal or compliance understanding, unsafe data use, collection of private-citizen information, and work that displaced core responsibilities.

The message establishes that these questions arose before the June 22 project discussion and the June 24 skill handoff. It does not establish a decision about the dashboard's long-term location or ownership.

## Legal connection

The July 7 message forwards Doug's June 25 note to Legal. It frames the purpose as bringing Legal further into AI literacy, tool development, and staff-facing guidance. The note asks Legal to address ten questions about review triggers, data and terms, legal-adjacent outputs, named individuals, entity boundaries, hosting, disclaimers, incident escalation, and an accessible longer-form resource.

It does not establish that Legal approved the originating project, adopted the review triggers, or resolved every risk.
