# DPI-007 Evidence Addendum

## Portal technical-trainer capacity experiment

Doug reports that he hired a technical trainer to address an adoption gap: E&T was expected to train staff on the portal without direct portal knowledge. He describes the hire as a higher-risk capacity experiment and cannot yet assess whether it paid off.

This is a detailed account. The trainer's identity, timing, reporting relationship, job scope, curriculum, training outputs, uptake, and outcome have not yet been recovered.

## Evidence boundary

Keep individual employment information, compensation, performance details, internal training materials, portal architecture, and user data private. Do not describe the hire as a successful achievement until the result is anchored.

## Next evidence question

Who was the technical trainer, and approximately when did Doug hire them? That will make a targeted search for the role request, description, training materials, feedback, and review records possible.
