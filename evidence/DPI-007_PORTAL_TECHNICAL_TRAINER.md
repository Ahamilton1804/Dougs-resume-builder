# DPI-007 Evidence Addendum

## Portal technical-trainer capacity experiment

Doug reports that he recruited Emily Keenan from i360 and hired her to provide technical training across AFP. The hire was intended to address an adoption gap: E&T was expected to train staff on the portal without direct portal knowledge. He describes it as a higher-risk capacity experiment.

Doug reports that Emily initially reported directly to him and that he stopped managing her around 2025. He describes the first year as rocky because E&T partners excluded her from work, particularly portal training. He reports that an associated portal-adoption project began well, then lost momentum and stalled during E&T reorganizations late in 2025. He reports that attention shifted toward evaluating a potential CRM replacement. He was not closely involved in those conversations, and no replacement decision had been made. In his assessment, uncertainty about whether the portal would be retained weakened interest in further training investment. These are detailed accounts. They describe operating conditions, not a completed result or a decided technology direction.

The locally available `i360 Ideal State Doc.docx` has core metadata identifying Pia Clarke as creator, an April 23, 2025 creation timestamp, and a June 10, 2025 modification by Ross Gardiner. It names Doug and Emily in a Portal Knowledge working group intended to improve i360 adoption, onboarding, training, and state-team role clarity. It also assigns Doug and Emily joint work on post-orientation effectiveness surveys and portal-role RRE work. This confirms an active cross-functional training and adoption effort in 2025. It does not establish the original hire, direct reporting period, interpersonal issues, subsequent E&T reorganization, or project outcome.

## Portal Knowledge Working Group Planner record

Doug reports that he started the Portal Knowledge Working Group and built a detailed Microsoft Planner project to address weak portal knowledge retention and make the case for continuing education beyond the approximately four-hour onboarding session. This is detailed account.

A live Planner review on September 7, 2026 found two plans with near-identical titles, both created February 9, 2025. The more detailed `Portal Knowledge Working Group` plan has five buckets: Training Optimization, Role-Based Expectations, Onboarding Optimization, i360 Adoption, and Phase 1. It contains 39 tasks created from February 9 through March 19, 2025. Nine are marked complete, five are in progress, and 25 have not started. The board includes work to audit training and usage, survey adoption challenges, map onboarding, compare training participation with adoption, pilot revised training in lower-adoption states, develop self-guided materials, establish role expectations and accountability, and create ongoing measurement.

This directly establishes an active, structured project surface with defined work and recorded status. Planner access does not establish that Doug created the plan, the substance behind completed task markers, participant behavior, or durable adoption results. The near-identical second plan should be treated as a possible earlier draft or duplicate until its relationship to the detailed plan is recovered.
