# AI-004 Evidence Addendum

## Workshop design and delivery approach

Doug reports that the April 2026 Microsoft and AFP workshops were designed to give Microsoft an accurate read on AFP's generative-AI maturity and to give staff a practical starting point. He used the prior summer's staff AI survey as a guide, worked with leadership to establish alignment, and participated in a dry run of the workshop material before delivery.

The workshops addressed a user population with widely different levels of AI familiarity. Doug reports choosing a middle-ground design centered on basic tool walkthroughs because AFP did not yet have a clear inventory of staff tool usage, widespread Enterprise ChatGPT access, or a common baseline beyond Copilot Chat availability. The existing planning materials document responsible-use and prompting instruction, peer stories, and hands-on Copilot Chat scenarios.

Doug reports finding staff members to share their own AI work examples. The purpose was to give staff a credible answer to the practical question, "How do I use AI in my role?" The quality and applicability of those stories varied. The record does not establish that the sessions created durable role-specific adoption or measured workflow improvement.

## What this source supports

- Doug brought governance, basic tool instruction, and role-relevant examples into the sessions.
