## EPS product ownership and people development

Doug estimates that he hired Rebecca Kush from the Legal function into a new EPS Product Owner role in approximately 2023. He reports that she entered without prior product-owner experience, developed into a high performer, and grew her responsibility for EPS. Rebecca reported directly to Doug. He reports that they met often, that he taught her Scrum and the product process, and that he helped establish the role before giving her space to own work day to day. He recalls that her prior supervisor, Jeff Ogar, encouraged her to take the career change.

This is a detailed account of the hire, development relationship, original experience level, timing, and prior-supervisor conversation.
