# DPI-006 Evidence Addendum

## EPS product ownership and people development

Doug reports that he hired Rebecca Kush from the Legal function into a new EPS Product Owner role in approximately 2023. He reports that she entered without prior product-owner experience, developed into a high performer, and grew her responsibility for EPS. Doug identifies his contribution as selecting her and supporting her development while giving her room to operate rather than managing her day-to-day work. He recalls that her prior supervisor, Jeff Ogar, encouraged her to take the career change.

This is a detailed account of the hire, development relationship, original experience level, timing, and prior-supervisor conversation.

## Available corroboration

`RRE Kush 2024.docx` has core metadata identifying Rebecca Kush as creator, with a November 18, 2022 creation timestamp and a December 11, 2024 modification by Doug. It describes a Product Owner role for EPS: translating stakeholder needs into requirements, setting product vision, managing and prioritizing a backlog, communicating delivery progress, helping users understand EPS, and maintaining product and engagement information. The 2022 core timestamp may indicate an early role draft. It does not establish that Rebecca held the role at that date or identify the hiring decision.

Doug-authored 2025 performance-review and compensation-request drafts describe Rebecca's later EPS work, including platform operations, backlog discipline, cross-functional delivery, and a Marcomms workflow project. These documents corroborate that Doug was assessing, supporting, and advocating for her later performance. They do not independently establish a 2023 hire, direct day-to-day management, or every underlying project outcome.

A user-supplied `Rebecca Kush - Role History.md` is a discovery map produced through Cowork. It points to Rebecca's 2023 self-review, 2024 role record, 2023 accomplishments, and later review materials. The underlying documents remain the recovery targets. The compilation itself is not evidence.

## Evidence boundary

Keep employment history, compensation, performance review content, compliance background, internal systems, project details, and individual development information private. Future external language may describe building product-owner capacity and developing product talent after the hiring decision and results are better anchored.
