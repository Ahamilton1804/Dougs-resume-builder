# Resume Handoff Questions

Doug asked the assistant on his other machine to supply the working decisions behind this career project. Read `README.md`, `evidence/SESSION_STATE.md`, `evidence/CAREER_EVIDENCE.md`, `evidence/CAREER_MAP.md`, `resumes/BASE_RESUME_DRAFT_NOTES.md`, and the current Word draft in `resumes/` before answering. Add a concise response at `evidence/RESUME_HANDOFF_RESPONSE.md` and commit it to the private repo.

This handoff should capture conclusions, decision rationale, source pointers, and unresolved questions. Summarize the useful results of prior discussions rather than copying chat transcripts or model deliberation. Keep raw AFP files, internal metrics, personnel details, sensitive examples, credentials, and screenshots out of Git. Use the ledger's exact evidence labels: **Confirmed**, **Detailed account**, **Lead**, and **Do not use externally**. If a source was not directly inspected, call it a lead.

## Questions

1. **What resume work already exists?** Identify any actual resume drafts, versions, or exports on the other machine by path, date, and format. Distinguish them from the six-page `Career_Evidence_and_Resume_Readiness_Inventory.docx` sent to Jill on October 15, 2026. State which version, if any, Doug reviewed or sent to someone.

2. **What target and length decisions did Doug make?** Summarize the role types, level, sectors, and responsibilities he preferred or rejected, including any reason to use one or two pages. Separate Doug's stated preferences from assistant recommendations. Note any job descriptions that shaped the target.

3. **Which claims earned space, and why?** For the AI Strategy and Product Implementation chapters, identify the five to eight strongest resume claims. For each, give Doug's distinct action, concrete output or result, source pointer and date, evidence label, safe external wording, and the stronger wording that was rejected or deferred. Prioritize decisions that affected the current Word draft.

4. **What has changed since the September 7 evidence handoff?** Report any direct new evidence about governance publication or approval, staff use of guidance, AI pilots and enablement, source-backed intelligence prototypes, product ownership, people development, iPad remediation, or other material work. For each, say whether the evidence establishes construction, circulation, adoption, a decision, or an outcome. Do not upgrade a claim based on a self-authored portfolio summary alone.

5. **What should change in the current two-page draft?** Review `resumes/Doug_Gardner_AI_Enablement_Base_Resume.docx` against the sources available there. List the most important cuts, additions, and wording corrections, with a brief reason for each. Flag any line whose attribution or external safety is weaker than the draft suggests.

6. **What chronology or education details are better sourced there?** Check the 2015 and 2025 AFP role transitions, the pre-AFP roles and schooling, and the short jobs between campaigns. Give exact dates only when a reliable record supports them. State when a broad year range is the honest choice.

7. **What decisions still need Doug?** List no more than five, ordered by how much each would improve the resume. Ask only for judgments or facts that cannot be recovered from existing records. Include any known preferences about tone, format, and contact information.

## Response format

Use short sections matching the seven questions. End with a **source register**: one line per source actually used, with its path or URL, date, what it supports, and any access or confidentiality limit. State the date of the handoff. Keep the response concise enough that another assistant can use it in one sitting.
